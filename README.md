# BitLife - Life Simulator Game

A free, open-source BitLife-inspired life simulation game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features

- **Character Creation**: Create your character with a name, gender, and starting country
- **Life Progression**: Age from 18 to 100, with random life events each year
- **Stats System**: Track Health, Happiness, Intelligence, and Looks
- **Money Management**: Earn and spend money on activities and choices
- **Career Choices**: School, work, and career development paths
- **Relationships**: Build and manage romantic relationships
- **Activities**: Study, party, gamble, invest, and more
- **Save/Load**: Auto-save your progress using browser storage
- **Random Events**: Unexpected life events that impact your stats

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click "New Game"
3. Create your character
4. Make life choices each year
5. See how your life unfolds!

## Installation

No installation needed! Just download the files and open `index.html` in any modern web browser.

### Files Included
- `index.html` - Main game page
- `style.css` - Game styling
- `game.js` - Game logic and mechanics

## Game Mechanics

### Stats
- **Health**: Decreases over time, increased by exercise and rest
- **Happiness**: Affected by your choices and activities
- **Intelligence**: Improved through study and education
- **Looks**: Affects relationship chances

### Actions
- **Pass Year**: Advance to the next year of your life
- **School**: Invest in education to boost intelligence
- **Work**: Find employment to earn money
- **Relationships**: Date, fall in love, or stay single
- **Activities**: Study, party, gamble, or invest your money

### Life Events
Random events occur each year that can:
- Give you money
- Affect your health
- Improve or harm relationships
- Present career opportunities
